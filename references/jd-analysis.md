# JD Analysis

Analyze the job description to extract key information that will guide project design. Analyze the challenges facing the company and the key competencies sought in candidates based on the job description.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Company & Product Understanding

### 2. Job Responsibilities and Objectives

### 3. Required Skills Extraction

### 4. Distinguishing Factors

What makes a candidate stand out for this specific role?

Construct a radar chart of role-specific capabilities that provide a competitive edge.


### 5. Output Template

```markdown
## JD Analysis Report

### Company Overview
[Company context and products]

### Job Responsibilities and Objectives
[Job responsibilities and objectives]

### Must-Have Skills
1. **Skill 1**
2. **Skill 2**

### Nice-to-Have Skills
1. **Skill 1**

### Description of Recommended Project Experience

Provide one or two descriptions of project experience that demonstrate the candidate's suitability for the position (approximately 200 words each).

```
